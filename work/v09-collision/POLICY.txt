Opem Amalur v0.9 authored movement policy

This is a new approximate controller over recovered render triangles. It is not
the original game's physics, collision asset interpretation, navigation, player
body, camera, or movement constants. Render meshes can include decorative solids,
missing collision surfaces, overlapping parts, roofs, and one-sided artwork; this
controller treats all admitted triangles as two-sided solids.

Coordinates are unchanged, Z up. Defaults assume 100 units per metre: capsule
radius32, height170, eye suggested at153 above feet, speed240/s, gravity980/s2,
step25, floor snap6, skin0.25, maximum slope45 degrees. These are authored policy
choices. No jump, crouch, swimming, animations, doors, actor collision, triggers,
quest actions, or native game callback is implied.

The scene is copied into an immutable, median-split AABB tree. Each query admits
at most4096 candidate triangles. Mesh count, coordinate range, build work, tree
depth, query stack, sweep iterations, contact iterations and per-frame substeps
are bounded. Query/convergence failure freezes the entire requested frame and
returns an error. Host must display an unavailable/error state; do not silently
enable walking without a collision world. Discarded contact residual is safe
under-motion, not an attempt to force the capsule through nearby geometry.

Capsule/triangle separation includes triangle faces, edges, and vertices, and
movement uses conservative continuous advancement. Float contact positions retain
at least half the configured skin in final validation. Horizontal contact slides
against walls; steep surfaces cannot redirect walking upward. Step candidates
require an unobstructed lift, swept horizontal movement, and floor landing.
Rounded curb-edge landings also require a nearby elevated walkable tread.

Spawn is deterministic: finite walkable triangle centroids sorted by distance to
the explicit interior hint(-350,380,0), with vertical distance weighted fourfold.
Candidate triangle area, full standing clearance, ground contact and overhead
geometry within1000 units are required. Up to4096 candidates are tested. This is
an indoor policy to avoid choosing a roof; overhead geometry alone does not prove
that a location is an original game spawn point. Failure leaves state unchanged.

Grounded walking requires a walkable landing within the larger of step height
and floor snap. Lower supported treads in this range are snapped down; deeper
drop-offs and missing floors reject horizontal movement and count an edge block.
This conservative policy also prevents intentionally walking off a real cliff.
It does not manufacture replacement floors or claim the recovered mesh is whole.

Gravity applies to airborne states. Falling250 units below the last validated
grounded pose, or below scene minimum Z minus two radii, restores that pose and
reports recovery. This is a missing-geometry safeguard, not native falling rules.
Last safe means collision-clear and supported; it is not a gameplay checkpoint.

Host owns input/yaw/pitch and controller state on one worker. Desired movement is
world XY velocity. Host may call at1/120 second; the library itself subdivides
accepted frames of up to0.1 second and clamps desired speed. Changing world/config
requires spawning anew. A failed step leaves the previous controller byte-for-byte
unchanged. Looking around may continue while walking is unavailable.

Validation entry points (execute on Windows):
  build_collision.cmd
  walk_scene.exe <path-to-OMSC07-scene.bin>
Synthetic tests exercise specific geometry and fail-closed policies. The real
scene CLI repeats spawn and performs eight six-second straight walking trials
with clearance checks at each frame. These tests do not establish all-scene
navigation, native physics equivalence, game completion, or comfortable controls.
