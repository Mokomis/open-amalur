Explicit module identity selection — v08 preview

export_scene_modules_v08.py accepts repeatable --select-module NAME=SHA256.
This chooses a byte identity already indexed under the exact wrapper name.
It never guesses case aliases, archive mount order, newest-file precedence,
or semantic equivalence between variants. Duplicate selection arguments,
invalid names/hashes, absent selected identities, ParentUtils pin conflicts,
and selections outside the exported closure fail explicitly. Existing source,
module-count and total-byte bounds and pinned ParentUtils contract remain.

All candidates remain in module-inventory.json ambiguous entries. A selected
ambiguity has resolution=explicit_preview_selection, selected_input_sha256,
and a reason explaining the preview-only identity choice. Unresolved entries
remain resolution=unresolved. unresolved_ambiguous contains only the latter.
explicit_module_selections lists each exact selected identity and all omitted
identities. A caller must not equate a nonempty ambiguous list with an
unresolved ambiguity now; inspect resolution or unresolved_ambiguous.
