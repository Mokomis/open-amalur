

v0.33 findings (2026-11-01). Each was proven from game data or shader disassembly in outputs/scene-v19/shaders.
Correction: the v0.28 and v0.30 moving results were taken on broken test walks and a world with wrong terrain. Use only results from the route walk (debug.opem.tour with actors/route.bin through exploration_update).
TERRAIN: heights are signed int16; only 65535 marks a hole. Chunk key is the map position; head of each chunk gives tile width; 21 chunks had gone unread. terrain_fill2.py recovered them. Fit k and z0 stand: actor heights median -1 cm.
RIGS: every fab has one joint table per surface in file order, including lower detail copies (palettes() in build_character.py and build_actors.py). The old longest-table reading mis-skinned multi-surface models. Inverse binds must come from the rig, for the mapped joint. Face joints absent from the rig follow the nearest ancestor.
PLAYER: Player motion set uses human_idle_01, human_walk_01, human_run_01; sword idle human_sword_idle_01; swings human_sword_attack_{x,xx,xxx,xxxx}_02, reaction human_norreacfrt, death human_Death_01. The _01 swings carry 52 joints and do NOT decode with this rig (joints 11-14,16,33 wrong, 52+ NaN); cause not understood. _01 and _02 are alternates, not recovery. Head fab 1362163 (varani male): 5 surfaces; skin texture is non-square.
NORMAL MAPS: x is r*a, y is g, z rebuilt. All world materials were wrong before this.
ALPHA TEST: green channel against per-material cutoff, word at +36 of mat_bxml (0 to 255).
FOG: keep = min(1, exp(-(a*w+b))), w = clip w, a = density/500000, b = fog_start; the shader's -1.442695 with exp (base 2) makes it base e. luminance_shift.bxml values: fog_density_scale 1.4, fog_distance_scale .6, environment_max .9, character_max .1, sky_max .9, sky_speed_scale 20, bloom_exposure_scale 1, bloom_min .4, bloom_max .8, bloom_strength_scale 1.1; they apply only through global weight +0xB8, whose normal-play value is still uncaptured.
SKYLINE: for_skyline materials are dot3alpha: lit, alpha tested, depth writing, shadow casting. No special treatment. Not compared with the original game by eye.
SPACES: slots 0x138/0x168/0x170 and 0x140/0x178/0x180 are extra instance groups (16-byte record with fab at +8; 80 and 84 byte records as usual) holding rivers, ponds, waterfalls, webs, clouds, smoke. Encounters: 60-byte records with an encounter id at +16; +0 pointer to count vec3 spawn points.
WATER: shaders river 709ff8ee6bd08c98 with vertex 292866647a27c82b; scrollingwaterfall ca77b6f297c27a99; emissivewithalphafog db5c4b1329799f28. Parameter order inferred from values, not names: hashes of names unknown. Reflection cube maps such as 743412_cubemap are not in the archives; the ambient cube stands in. Point lights add no highlight on water. Vertex alpha is byte stride-1.
SOUND: koarsoundbank tables and .ksdf files. FORK header; WAVEFORMATEX at 0x2c, MS ADPCM, blocks from 0x3e+cbSize to big-endian offset at 0x20. oa_adpcm_test matches ffmpeg exactly. Events are authored; motion tables not decoded. Levels are authored; forest bed is very quiet in data.
PATHS: oa_nav on a 2 m map from slope and collision (build_route.py, actors/nav.bin). The game's path_set_bxml files (1467) are unused.
TEST AIDS: debug.opem.tour, .fight (tap period in 10 ms), .slow (percent game speed), .at "x y z". Tablet blocks screenrecord; use .slow with screenshots (scripts video.sh, capture_motion.sh, lookat.sh). rig_check.py gives rest-pose error, gaze and side renders without the tablet.
Result, v0.33 with water, 790 actors and 932 lights at 2520x1680, before sound: vsync 10 min p95 16.67, worst frame 32.17, GPU mean 7.94, max 10.61, no frame over 40 ms; uncapped mean 9.3, p95 10.54. Thermal 0-1 at 60 fps, 2 uncapped. Route covers 3.3 km, 29 legs. Player was not attacking in that run.
